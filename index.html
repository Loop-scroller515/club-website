WHO WE ARE

About TechForge

Forging the Future, One Project at a Time

TechForge is a passionate community of engineers, designers, and innovators who believe in learning by doing. Founded by students who wanted to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world application.

We provide hands-on workshops, collaborative projects, and competitive opportunities across three core domains: Computer-Aided Design, Electronics, and Mechanical Engineering.

Hands-on Workshop Every Week
Industry Mentorship Programs
Competition Team Support
Free Tools & Resources

Our Vision

To create a generation of problem-solvers who can design, build, and innovate without limits.

Our Mission

Making technical education accessible, practical, and exciting for every student.
