WHO WE ARE

About HumanoidX

Where Human Minds Build Machine Futures

HumanoidX represents the perfect fusion — half neural network, half circuit board. We are a community of engineers, programmers, and creators who believe the next frontier of technology lies at the intersection of human creativity and machine intelligence.

From building humanoid robots to designing intelligent circuits, from CAD modeling complex mechanisms to programming embedded systems — we do it all, hands-on, together.

Weekly Hands-on Workshops
Industry Mentorship
Robotics Competitions
Free Tools & Lab Access

Our Vision

To create the next generation of innovators who can think like humans and build like machines.

Our Mission

Bridging the gap between biological intelligence and artificial systems through practical engineering.
